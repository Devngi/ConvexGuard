Dataset & Features

Source: Credit Card Fraud Detection dataset (ULB / Kaggle). Two days of European card transactions, September 2013.

Source columns

ColumnMeaning
TimeSeconds elapsed since the first transaction in the dataset (data covers 2 days).
V1 to V28Anonymized features. The bank applied PCA (principal component analysis) to the original card-transaction details (such as merchant, location and card information) and released only the resulting components for privacy. They are uncorrelated with each other, are already centred, and individual meanings are not disclosed.
AmountTransaction amount in dollars.
ClassTarget label: 1 = fraud, 0 = legitimate.

Engineered features

FeatureFormulaReason
LogAmountln(1 + Amount)Tames the heavy right skew of raw transaction amounts.
Hourfloor(Time / 3600) mod 24Captures the hour of day, which correlates with fraud patterns.

The model uses 30 features: V1 to V28, LogAmount and Hour.

Because V1 to V28 cannot be mapped back to real payment fields, the form therefore asks for the key signals directly.

Mathematical derivation

1. Soft-margin primal with class-dependent penalties

Fraud detection is highly imbalanced. We penalise missed frauds (false negatives) more heavily than false alarms by setting C+ > C−.

subject to   yi(w⊤xi + b) ≥ 1 − ξi,   ξi ≥ 0.

This is the unconstrained convex form minimised directly by the subgradient solver.

3. Lagrangian and KKT conditions

Introducing dual variables αi ≥ 0 and μi ≥ 0:

The KKT stationarity conditions require:

  • w = ∑ αi yi xi  (S1)
  • ∑ αi yi = 0  (S2)
  • Ci = αi + μi,   μi ≥ 0  (S3)
  • αi[yi fi − 1 + ξi] = 0  (CS1)
4. Dual problem

Maximising the Lagrangian over w and b gives the dual:

subject to 0 ≤ αi ≤ Ci and ∑ αi yi = 0.

5. Convexity and strong duality

The primal objective is convex: the quadratic term has a positive-semidefinite Hessian and the hinge loss is convex. Every local minimum is therefore global.

Strong duality (zero duality gap) holds because Slater's condition is satisfied: w = 0, b = 0, ξi = 1 is a strictly feasible interior point. Therefore P* − D* = 0 at the optimum.
